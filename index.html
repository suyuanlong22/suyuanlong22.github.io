Research interests: environmental engineering, urban noise, machine learning, and air quality.

I am a PhD student in Environmental Engineering at Washington University in St. Louis. My work focuses on source-specific urban environmental exposure, including noise and air pollution.

I use audio sensing, machine learning, and environmental data analysis to study how urban environments affect public health.
