About Me

I am a Ph.D. candidate in Energy, Environmental & Chemical Engineering at Washington University in St. Louis, advised by Prof. Jay Turner. I received my B.E. in Environmental Engineering from Tsinghua University in 2024.

My work uses machine learning to analyze audio, sensor, microscopy, and geospatial data. I am interested in multimodal learning, audio analysis, computer vision, and spatiotemporal modeling.

Selected Research

Semantic Decomposition of Large-Scale Urban Audio
PyTorch · CLAP · AudioSep · HPC · torchaudio
  • Built an HPC inference pipeline for 11,000 hours of urban field audio.
  • Created a 12-class benchmark from 8,444 annotated clips; reached 99.0% supervised accuracy and 96.2% zero-shot accuracy.
  • Used source-separation outputs to estimate source-specific noise exposure across sites and seasons.
Multimodal Spatiotemporal Temperature Modeling
LightGBM · Swin Transformer · PCA · Geospatial Data
  • Combined field sensors, weather data, and high-resolution imagery for site- and hour-level temperature prediction.
  • Reduced two 1,024-dimensional image embeddings to 64 principal components retaining 98% of the variance.
  • Reached 0.33°C RMSE on withheld periods and 0.34–0.52°C in leave-station-out validation.
Multimodal Single-Particle Classification
XGBoost · Microscopy · Elemental Composition · Morphology
  • Built a pipeline that aligns microscopy images, elemental measurements, and particle morphology.
  • Used literature-based rules to create high-confidence silver labels from imbalanced raw data.
  • Trained XGBoost models with 28 engineered multimodal features and 10-fold spatial cross-validation.
Dust-Event Detection in Sensor Time Series
Python · NMF · Time-Series Analysis
  • Developed an unsupervised event-detection method using 17 months of particulate-matter sensor data.
  • Reached 84% sensitivity and 89% precision against reported dust events.
